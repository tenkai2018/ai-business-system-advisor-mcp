# Sample Mini Business System Review

## Business Snapshot

A B2B service agency helps small ecommerce brands improve landing pages and conversion performance. The team spends too much time researching leads, preparing proposals, and writing follow-up messages.

## Likely Bottlenecks

- Lead research is manual and inconsistent.
- Proposal preparation depends heavily on expert time.
- Customer-facing claims need review before sending.

## AI Opportunity Areas

- Lead research brief
- Sales call preparation
- Proposal draft assistant

## Trust And Control Risks

- AI should not promise outcomes without human approval.
- Pricing, scope, and customer-facing commitments should be reviewed.
- Low-confidence research should be flagged before sales use.

## Recommended First Workflow

Lead research and qualification with human-approved proposal drafting.

## Example Success Metrics

- Qualified sales calls booked
- Proposal cycle time
- Human edit rate
- Lead-to-call conversion rate

## Important Note

This is a fictional example and a first-pass review only.
