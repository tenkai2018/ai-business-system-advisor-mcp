# Sample Mini Business System Review

## Business Snapshot

A service agency sells conversion and landing page work to ecommerce brands. Sales capacity is constrained by manual lead research, inconsistent qualification, and proposal drafting.

## Likely Bottlenecks

- Lead research takes too long before a sales call.
- Proposal quality depends on expert availability.
- Qualification criteria are not consistently applied.
- Customer-facing claims need clearer approval rules.

## AI Opportunity Areas

- Lead research and qualification brief
- Sales call preparation
- Proposal section drafting from approved language
- Follow-up preparation after human review

## Trust And Control Risks

Risk level is medium. The workflow can affect revenue, brand trust, scope, and customer expectations.

AI should prepare research, summarize signals, and draft first-pass materials. A human should review lead fit, scope, pricing, claims, and final customer-facing messages before anything is sent.

## Recommended First Workflow

AI prepares a lead research and qualification brief, then drafts proposal sections from approved language. A human approves the lead quality, scope, pricing, claims, and final message.

## Example Success Metrics

- Qualified calls booked
- Proposal cycle time
- Human edit rate
- Lead-to-call conversion
- Proposals requiring major revision

## Missing Information

- Ideal customer criteria
- Approved claims
- Disallowed claims
- Proposal rules
- Escalation triggers
- Success metrics

## Important Note

This is a fictional example and a first-pass review only.
