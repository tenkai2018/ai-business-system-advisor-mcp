# Sample Intake Packet

## Business Context

- Business type: B2B service agency
- Target customer: small ecommerce brands
- Core offer: landing page and conversion optimization
- Goal: book more qualified sales calls

## Current Problem

The team spends too much time on manual lead research, proposal drafting, and follow-up preparation.

## Current Workflow

Research leads manually, prepare sales notes, write proposals, and send follow-up messages.

## Likely Bottlenecks

- Manual research slows sales capacity.
- Proposal drafting consumes expert time.
- Qualification criteria may be inconsistent.

## Recommended First Workflow

AI prepares a lead research and qualification brief. A human reviews lead quality, approved claims, pricing, scope, and customer-facing messages before anything is sent.

## Missing Information

- Ideal customer criteria
- Approved proposal language
- Disallowed claims
- Escalation rules
- Success metrics

## Important Note

This is a fictional example and should be adapted with sanitized business context.
